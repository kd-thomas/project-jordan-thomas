THE PRODUCT

JORDAN

Jobsite Oncoming Risk Detection & Alert Network

A work-zone safety platform being designed to help road crews report danger, warn one another, identify recurring risk and turn field observations into action.

Currently in development.

CORE PURPOSE

Report. Alert. Detect. Act.

01

Report

Workers quickly report speeding vehicles, work-zone intrusions, near misses, hazards, crashes and injuries.

02

Alert

Immediate danger can trigger real-time warnings for the crew.

03

Detect

Repeated reports become risk intelligence instead of isolated events.

04

Act

Reports support corrective action, escalation and documented safety evidence.

THE PRINCIPLE

No data abyss.

Every report should create an immediate safety function, management action, escalation path or usable evidence.

Useful from the first crew.

JORDAN does not need statewide adoption to provide value.

  • Worker hazard reports
  • Crew warnings
  • Foreman visibility
  • Corrective-action tracking
  • Risk history
