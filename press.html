LEGISLATIVE ADVOCACY

Christina Greenwood's testimony in support of House Bill 82.

Jordan's mother, Christina Greenwood, provided proponent testimony before the Ohio House in support of House Bill 82.

“The hole that Jordan's death has created in our family will never be filled, but protecting the lives that grow around it is my call to action.”

Her testimony calls for greater safety and accountability in construction zones so other road workers can return home to their families.
