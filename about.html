ABOUT THE PROJECT

One mission. Two parts.

THE PROJECT

Project Jordan Thomas

Project Jordan Thomas is the broader public initiative created in Jordan's memory.

Its mission can extend beyond software into advocacy, education, research, awareness and partnerships focused on protecting road workers.

THE PRODUCT

JORDAN

JORDAN is the technology product being developed under Project Jordan Thomas.

Its purpose is to turn real-time field observations into warnings, evidence, risk intelligence and action.

CREATED & DEVELOPED BY

Kay Thomas
