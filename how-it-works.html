HOW JORDAN WORKS

From field observation to safety action.

The goal is to make reporting easy for workers while giving foremen and safety leaders meaningful information.

1

Worker reports danger

The worker identifies what happened. JORDAN can capture location, time, work zone and crew information automatically.

2

Crew gets warned

If the threat is immediate, workers can receive a safety alert.

3

Foreman gets context

Reports become part of a live picture of what is happening across the active work zone.

4

Patterns emerge

Multiple reports can reveal recurring speeding, vehicle intrusion, near misses or location-specific risk.

5

Corrective action is documented

Foremen and safety teams can document what was changed in response to the risk.

6

Information can move outside JORDAN

Relevant information can support company escalation, transportation-agency communication, law enforcement or formal safety reporting when appropriate.
